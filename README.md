# 🚗 BMW Global Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Summary

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes BMW’s global sales performance, revenue trends, and customer preferences across regions, fuel types, and vehicle models.

The dashboard enables business stakeholders to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and understand evolving automotive trends such as the shift toward hybrid and electric vehicles.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Evaluate overall sales performance and revenue trends
* Identify top-performing BMW models
* Analyze demand by fuel type and transmission
* Compare regional sales contributions
* Track year-over-year sales performance
* Understand market transition toward sustainable mobility

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Sales:** 253 Million Vehicles
* **Total Revenue:** 19 Trillion
* **Average Vehicle Price:** 75.03K
* **Total Models Sold:** 50K

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🚘 Model Performance

* Premium and performance models contribute significantly to sales.
* Strong demand observed across luxury and sport segments.

### ⛽ Fuel Type Trends

* Hybrid vehicles lead adoption.
* Electric vehicle demand is comparable to petrol and diesel.
* Indicates a clear transition toward eco-friendly mobility.

### 🌍 Regional Sales Distribution

* Sales are evenly distributed across:

  * Asia
  * Europe
  * North America
  * Middle East
  * Africa
  * South America
* Demonstrates BMW’s strong global market presence.

### 📅 Sales Trend Analysis

* Sales remain stable between 16M–18M annually.
* Peak performance observed around recent years.
* Minor dips suggest potential supply chain or market factors.

### ⚙ Transmission Insights

* Automatic transmission usage is increasing globally.
* Manual transmission remains relevant in emerging markets.
